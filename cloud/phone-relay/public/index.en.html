Start on the computer, continue on your phone.

In CodeBee on your computer, open “Phone connection”, click “Generate pairing code”, then scan the QR code with your phone — or simply open the link copied from the computer.

The link pairs automatically and opens your own CodeBee — no need to type a pairing code.
